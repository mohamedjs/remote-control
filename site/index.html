Frequently Asked Questions

Do my phone and computer need to be on the same WiFi?

Yes! Remote Control communicates directly over your local area network (LAN) for maximum privacy and zero latency.

Is internet access required?

No internet connection is required for trackpad, keyboard, or deck controls. Only the optional voice intent feature queries the Groq API.

How do I launch the desktop window?

Simply run `remote-control dashboard` in your terminal or click the application icon from your system menu.
